Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Setwl is a free, crowdsourced record of what it is actually like to live on a particular street. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do about it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

Setwl is operated from Nigeria and is subject to the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. If you only read one section, read “What is public”.

What is public

Reviews on Setwl are public by design. When you post a review, the following is visible to anyone, without signing in:

  • Your ratings for power, water, security, drainage and serenity
  • Anything you wrote in the review text
  • Any street tag, rent range and property type you entered
  • The area the review is about, and the date it was posted

Your name and email address are never shown next to your review. Reviews are displayed without any author name. Please do not put your own name, phone number, house number or anyone else's personal details into the review text, because that text is published exactly as you write it.

What we collect

When you just browse

You are signed in anonymously in the background so the site can load data. This does not identify you. We store a copy of area data in your browser's local storage so the site loads faster on your next visit; you can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

When you post a review

You sign in with Google. We receive and store your Google account identifier and email address. The identifier is attached to your review so the same person cannot review the same area repeatedly. Your email is used to contact you about your own contributions. Neither is displayed publicly.

When you request an area

If you ask us to add an area, we store the area details and, if you choose to give it, your email address. The email field is optional. We use it for one thing: to tell you when the area you asked for goes live, or when a review you left alongside the request is published.

Rain Checks

If you report current weather for an area, we store the condition you selected, the area, the time, and your account identifier. Rain Checks expire from the live view after a short period.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share your email with landlords, agents or advertisers.
  • We do not add you to a marketing list because you requested an area. If we ever start sending general updates, it will be because you separately opted in, and every message will let you stop.
  • We do not show your identity next to your review.

Who else can see it

Setwl runs on Google Firebase, so data is stored and processed on Google Cloud infrastructure. Sign-in is handled by Google. We do not use advertising trackers.

How long we keep things

Reviews are kept for as long as the area exists on Setwl, because the value of the record is that it accumulates over time. Area requests are kept until they are approved or rejected, plus a reasonable period afterwards. You can ask for your data to be removed at any time.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act you can ask us to:

  • tell you what we hold about you
  • correct something that is wrong
  • delete your account data
  • delete a review you posted
  • stop using your email to contact you

To do any of these, email hello@setwl.com.ng from the address you signed up with. We will respond within 30 days.

If you believe we have mishandled your data, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Children

Setwl is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change is significant, say so on the site.
